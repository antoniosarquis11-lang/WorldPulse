# WorldPulse story format rules

All current and future WorldPulse stories must follow this detailed format.

## Required structure

TITLE

To go further:

[Source 1]
[Source 2]
[Source 3]

Note: This story was generated with AI — model: GPT-5.5 Thinking — and reviewed by humans. It is designed to explain complex economic and geopolitical topics in a simple, accessible way.

Quick Insight — estimated time: X min

[Clear, accessible and substantial explanation.]

Key takeaway: [Takeaway]

Medium Story — estimated time: X min

01 — [Section title]

[Developed explanation: what happens and why it matters.]

[Developed mechanism/example/consequence.]

Deep Dive — estimated time: X min

01 — [Section title]

[Expanded analytical explanation with causes, mechanisms, data context, risks and long-term implications.]

Key takeaway: [Takeaway]

## Rules

- No Story Globe Mode.
- No time ranges; only specific estimates.
- Medium Story is the main explainer version, not an outline.
- Medium Story numbered sections should be developed, generally 2 paragraphs per section.
- Deep Dive sections should be longer, analytical and mechanism-driven.
- Quick Insight should be beginner-friendly but still substantial.

## Applied to earlier stories
The 10 earlier stories follow the same finalized detailed format as the newest 20 stories: Medium Story sections are developed explanatory sections, not outline notes; Deep Dive sections use longer analytical paragraphs; Quick Insight remains substantial; Story Globe Mode is removed.
